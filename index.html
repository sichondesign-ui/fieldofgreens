Release 03 — The complete site, every page.Latest

2026 · 10 · 15

The full sitemap, blocked out end to end: refined versions of the Round-1 pages plus nine new designs — Locations, Catering, About, Order Online, Contact, Gift Cards, Careers, the legal text-page template, and the 404. Everything is wired together as one browsable prototype: every navigation item, footer link, and call-to-action goes somewhere. The Order and Gift Cards store pickers are live on the page. Buttons that hand off to ordering partners (Toast, UberEats, EZCater) are intentionally inactive — those links get wired in the Wix Studio build. Where content awaits confirmation (careers roles, gift-card terms, legal copy), the page shows representative placeholder content.
