Our Story · West Palm Beach

It started with a promise.

2003
First kitchen · West Palm Beach
Since 2003

Cook everything from scratch, every day. No exceptions.

Field of Greens opened its first kitchen in West Palm Beach in 2003 with one rule, and it hasn't bent since: everything is made from scratch, every morning, in every store. The dressings, the soups, the roasted chicken, the granola — all of it.

Two decades later, that first kitchen has grown into a family of stores across Palm Beach County. The menu has grown with it. The rule hasn't.
