Our Story

From one kitchen in West Palm Beach.

Field of Greens opened its first kitchen in West Palm Beach in 2003 with a simple promise: cook everything from scratch, every day, no exceptions. Two decades — and ten locations across South Florida — later, the mantra painted on our wall is still exactly how we make your lunch.
