The licensing deck, before and after.

Left: the original Licensing Deck (1).pptx, 12 slides. Right: the rebuilt grayscale wireframe, now living as native slides in Figma. Every fact, figure, date, name, and the AUV disclaimer carries over verbatim — what changed is structure, sequence, and emphasis.

New-deck images are screenshots of the live Figma Slides file (Field of Greens Deck Wireframe). Dashed boxes are intentional photo placeholders, not missing content.

What changed, in one pass

The original told a good story in a scattered order: growth proof twice (slides 2 and 3), identity arriving late (slide 5), territory logic split across slides 4 and 9, and the deck's only urgency device buried in a caption. The rebuild resequences into a single argument — who we are → proof → people → why it travels → where → money → terms → support → path → ask — merges the duplicates, adds one new slide (the team), and moves the full press record to an appendix so nothing is deleted.
